# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Erik Jahn and Dominik Janzing for developing and sharing *Evaluating Bivariate Causal Statements Based on Mutual Compatibility*. The
paper's claim structure and this source workspace make it possible to connect
each claim to an evidence producer, retain the controls and limitations, and
keep the collection record honest about what is and is not reproduced.

This repository is an organizational record of a legacy source workspace. It
does not imply author endorsement of the historical results.
